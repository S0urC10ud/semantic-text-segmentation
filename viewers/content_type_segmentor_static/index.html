Acknowledgments

This work was carried out with extensive technical feedback from and many informative discussions with Dr. Yanick Fratantonio and Dr. Luca Invernizzi from Google Security Research (authors of Magika), who also provided generous access to Google Cloud compute resources. I am especially grateful for his detailed feedback on the accompanying master's thesis and for him supporting this academic collaboration.
