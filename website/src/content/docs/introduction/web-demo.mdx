Paste text or select an example. **Mamba is selected for its full-file context**; choose U-Net in Settings for faster inference. Hover or select a region in the composition bar to see its location, then inspect the highlighted text.

Inference runs locally. The page downloads model assets, but does not upload your input. This demo uses the **general checkpoints**, not the security-adapted models evaluated in the paper. Browser speed depends on your device and differs from the paper’s native-runtime benchmarks.
